Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026　· Last updated: September 4, 2026

中文版

"Jutou AI" ("the App", "we", or "us") is a Korean-drama information and watch-tracking tool. It offers drama browsing, AI conversational recommendations, a personal watchlist with per-episode check-ins, a weekly-update calendar, and local reminders. The App does not stream, download, or aggregate any video content, and does not link out to any third-party viewing service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over your information. Please read it before using the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Policy.

Key points: The App requires no sign-up and no login; there are no accounts. Your watchlist, check-in progress, reminder settings, and AI chat history are stored only on your current device by default — they are not uploaded to our servers, not tied to any identity, and not synced across devices. Only the "AI Picks" and "Weekly Calendar" features send any data to the server (see sections 1 and 4).

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information stored only on your device, which we never receive

The following data is written by the App directly into your device's app sandbox. It is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot read or back it up:

  • Watch-tracking data: the dramas you add to your watchlist, the category you choose (Watching / Want to watch / Finished / Dropped), per-episode check-in records, the reminder toggle, ordering, and the "X dramas tracked" / "X-day streak" statistics computed locally from them.
  • AI chat history: your past "AI Picks" conversations, kept only so you can review them in the App.

1.2 Information sent to the server when you use certain features

  • AI Picks input: the natural-language query text you type into "AI Picks" (e.g. "a sweet romance under 8 episodes"). How this text is handled is described in section 4.
  • IDs of dramas you are tracking: when you use the "Weekly Calendar", the App sends the list of drama IDs you are currently watching to the server solely to compute that week's update schedule on the fly. The server discards them immediately, does not store them, and does not tie them to any identifier.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Basic service logs: browsing drama data, using AI Picks, or using the Weekly Calendar sends requests to our server. For security and troubleshooting, the server records, for a limited time, necessary technical information such as request time, endpoint path, HTTP status code, and source IP address. These logs are not used for profiling or advertising; they are used only for security and operations and are rotated and cleared regularly.

1.4 Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or access:

  • accounts, usernames, passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, real names, or government-ID information (the App has no account system)
  • location
  • contacts, call logs, SMS
  • photos, camera, microphone
  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking identifier
  • health, biometric, or financial/payment information

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or social-login SDK. It contains no ads and no in-app purchases.

3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share necessary information with third parties only in the following cases:

  • Large-model service provider: to power "AI Picks", your query text is sent to a third-party large-language-model service. See section 4.
  • Infrastructure provider: the backend and database that serve drama data, AI Picks, and the Weekly Calendar are hosted on AWS. It processes data only on our instructions to provide compute and storage, and may not use it for its own purposes.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose necessary information when required by law or by a judicial or administrative authority.
  • Business transfer: in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction; we will require the recipient to continue to honor this Policy and will notify you in advance.

4. AI Picks and cross-border data transfer

Important: When you use "AI Picks", your query text is forwarded through our server to the third-party large-language-model provider DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.) to generate recommendations. That provider's servers are located in the People's Republic of China. If you are located elsewhere, this constitutes a cross-border data transfer.
  • Only the query text of the current turn is sent (in a follow-up, previous turns' text may be included for context). Because the App has no account system, the transmitted content does not include any account, user ID, or information that directly identifies you.
  • The third party's handling of the received text is governed by its own privacy policy: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html.
  • We do not store AI Picks input text on the server for analysis; the chat history is kept only on your device.
  • Do not enter any sensitive personal information into "AI Picks" (such as your real name, contact details, ID numbers, or location).

If you do not want this transfer to occur, do not use "AI Picks"; all other features (browsing, watchlist, check-ins, calendar, reminders) work normally without it.

5. Data storage and security

  • Your watch-tracking data and AI chat history are stored as files in the App's sandbox directory (Application Support), accessible only to the App, and deleted when the App is uninstalled. This data is not backed up to the cloud and is not synced between your devices.
  • All communication between the client and the server uses HTTPS encryption.
  • We follow the principle of data minimization: the server receives only what is necessary to run AI Picks and the Weekly Calendar.
  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no internet transmission or local storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security; please keep your device safe.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • The App has no accounts. Your watch-tracking data and AI chat history are stored entirely on your device and are kept until you delete them.
  • The App provides one-tap deletion under "Settings → Clear local data". After you confirm, your watchlist, check-in records, and AI chat history are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered; uninstalling the App also deletes them.
  • For security-audit and legal-compliance purposes, residual technical records in service logs are automatically cleared within the normal rotation cycle (generally no more than 30 days).
  • Deletion of historical query text already sent to the third-party large-model service is governed by that third party's data-retention policy.

7. Local push notifications

"Update reminders" are delivered via iOS local notifications: the reminder schedule is created and fired locally on your device (fixed at 20:00 daily). It does not go through our server and does not involve collecting a remote push token. You can turn notifications off at any time, in the App or in iOS Settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided us information without a guardian's consent, please contact us using section 12 and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you may:

  • Access and view: view all of your watch-tracking data and statistics in the App at any time.
  • Correct: change your watchlist, check-ins, and reminder settings in the App.
  • Delete: permanently delete all of your local data via "Settings → Clear local data" or by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw consent: stopping use of "AI Picks" withdraws consent to the related data transfer; turning off notifications withdraws the reminder authorization.

If you have any objection to our processing, contact us using section 12.

10. Third-party platform information

The "Where to watch" field on a drama's detail page shows the broadcasting platform's name as plain text only (e.g. "tvN", "Netflix"), with no links, and we do not share any of your information with those platforms. When you go to a platform to watch, your relationship with that platform and its data processing are governed by that platform's terms and privacy policy.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When a change takes effect, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page; for material changes, we will notify you prominently in the App. Continuing to use the App after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
